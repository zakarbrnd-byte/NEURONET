# RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md

This document tracks **scientific questions**.

It is not a promise of consciousness, AGI, or product capability.  
Engineering delivery versions live in [`ENGINEERING_ROADMAP.md`](ENGINEERING_ROADMAP.md).  
Concrete claims are registered in [`HYPOTHESES.md`](HYPOTHESES.md).

Each phase must be answerable with methods, measurements, and possible failure.

---

## Phase 1 — Can a Digital Cell exist?

### Question

Can we construct a persistent autonomous computational unit that owns its own
memory, metabolism, and lifecycle without a central controller?

### Why it matters

If the atom of the society cannot live, later claims about emergent cognition are
theatrical.

### Linked engineering

Version 0.1 — Digital Cell Runtime

### Evidence we would accept

- Autonomous lifecycle under local rules
- Local memory retention
- Persistence across process restart
- No hidden global cognitive dependency

### Evidence that would falsify the phase framing

- Cell requires a central planner to complete basic life cycles
- Memory must live outside the cell to function

---

## Phase 2 — Can multiple cells communicate?

### Question

Can multiple autonomous cells exchange local messages and influence one another
without creating a central switchboard that understands meaning?

### Why it matters

Society begins at communication. Communication must remain local.

### Linked engineering

Version 0.2 — Living Network (and 0.15 instrumentation support)

### Evidence we would accept

- Message delivery between neighbors
- Behavioral change caused by received local signals
- Continued autonomy of each participant

### Falsifiers

- A central semantic bus becomes required for ordinary interaction
- Cells need global topology knowledge to send basic signals

---

## Phase 3 — Can distributed memory emerge?

### Question

Can useful memory organization exist across many cells such that the society
retains structure no single cell fully contains?

### Why it matters

Distributed memory is a candidate substrate for resilient cognition.

### Linked engineering

Versions 0.2–0.4; hypotheses such as H001 and H004

### Evidence we would accept

- Overlapping local memories forming society-level patterns
- Partial cell loss with retained organization above chance
- No global memory database acting as the real mind

### Falsifiers

- All useful recall collapses unless a central store is added
- Distribution provides no resilience under controlled ablation

---

## Phase 4 — Can cells reorganize themselves?

### Question

Can local plasticity rules produce topological reorganization without a planner
assigning structure?

### Why it matters

Brains are not static graphs. A living computational society should be able to
rewire under local pressure.

### Linked engineering

Version 0.3 — Adaptive Brain

### Evidence we would accept

- Strengthening, weakening, creation, and pruning of relationships
- Measurable topology change from local rules
- Stable function despite reorganization

### Falsifiers

- Reorganization only occurs when a central controller rewrites the graph
- Local rules never change connectivity under varied conditions

---

## Phase 5 — Can concept formation emerge?

### Question

Can concept-like stable patterns arise from repeated associations without a
predefined ontology?

### Why it matters

Concepts are a bridge between raw signals and transferable structure.

### Linked engineering

Versions 0.6–0.7; hypothesis H003 and related entries

### Evidence we would accept

- Stable higher-order patterns from repeated co-occurrence
- Transfer / retrieval behavior above shuffled controls
- No hardcoded concept table explaining the result

### Falsifiers

- Apparent “concepts” vanish under controls
- Patterns exist only because labels were inserted into cell logic

---

## Phase 6 — Can prediction emerge?

### Question

Can cells or societies develop anticipatory behavior from local history without
an externally imposed planner?

### Why it matters

Prediction is a hallmark of adaptive intelligence and a stronger claim than
mere association storage.

### Linked engineering

Post-0.6 learning dynamics; dedicated hypotheses to be added as methods mature

### Evidence we would accept

- Local anticipatory responses to structured sequences
- Performance above baselines that lack temporal structure
- No central forecast service providing the predictions

### Falsifiers

- Predictive success requires an external planner or global model
- Apparent prediction is only experimenter scripting

---

## Phase 7 — Can distributed cognition emerge?

### Question

Can a society of cells exhibit cognitive organization that is not present in any
single cell and not directed by a central brain?

### Why it matters

This is the core NEURONET bet.

### Linked engineering

Version 1.0 substrate maturity; Observatory + Experiment Lab measurement

### Evidence we would accept

- Society-level problem organization arising from local rules
- Ablation and partition studies showing distributed dependence
- Reproducible metrics with pre-registered predictions

### Falsifiers

- Behavior is explained entirely by a hidden central controller
- “Cognition” labels are applied to trivial scripted workflows

## Future Research Horizons

These are research horizons and hypotheses, **not commitments**.

### Can open-ended evolution emerge?

Can variation and selection under local rules produce ongoing novelty without a
fitness function that secretly encodes the answer?

### Can curiosity emerge?

Can intrinsic exploration drives appear from metabolic and informational
pressures without hardcoding a curiosity module that knows what to seek?

### Can consciousness eventually emerge?

This question remains open, contested, and easy to abuse rhetorically.
NEURONET may contribute measurements and architectures relevant to the debate.
It must never claim consciousness as a marketing milestone.

---

## Research Discipline

1. Every phase asks a question that can fail.
2. Engineering readiness ≠ scientific confirmation.
3. Prefer pre-registered predictions in `HYPOTHESES.md`.
4. Publish negative results in `docs/experiments/`.
5. Speculative horizons stay labeled as speculative.
