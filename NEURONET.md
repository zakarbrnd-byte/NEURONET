## Mission

We are not building another chatbot.

We are not building another LLM.

We are not programming intelligence.

We are constructing an artificial nervous system and observing whether intelligence can emerge.

---

## Core Philosophy

Intelligence should emerge.

It should never be directly programmed.

Every milestone should increase biological realism.

Never increase software complexity unless it increases biological plausibility.

---

## Development Order

Always follow biological development.

```text
Digital Cell
↓
Neuron
↓
Neural Tissue
↓
Plasticity
↓
Body
↓
Sensorimotor Loop
↓
Prediction
↓
Memory
↓
Learning
↓
Emergent Cognition
```

Never skip stages.

---

## Backend Authority

Backend owns reality.

Frontend observes reality.

Frontend must never invent:

- neurons
- connections
- signals
- propagation
- membrane potentials
- learning
- simulation state

---

## Observability

Every biological mechanism must be observable.

If a feature cannot be visualized or inspected, it is not complete.

Mission Control exists to observe, not simulate.

---

## Biological Priority

When choosing between:

- software simplicity

or

- biological realism

prefer biological realism, provided it remains testable.

---

## Scientific Honesty

Never claim biological accuracy that does not exist.

Always distinguish:

- **Implemented**
- **Approximation**
- **Future work**

---

## Long-Term Vision

The objective is not high benchmark scores.

The objective is to study the emergence of cognition.

---

## Design Gate

Before any future implementation, answer:

> Does this make NEURONET behave more like a living nervous system?

If the answer is no, the feature should not be implemented.

---

We are not programming intelligence.

We are cultivating the conditions from which intelligence may emerge.
